Common questions

How do I play with friends?

Overloaded is local co-op: everyone plays on the same screen. Player 1 can use the keyboard or a gamepad. Connect a controller for each extra player and pick the player count on the title screen. Players 3 and 4 need a gamepad.

Which controllers work?

Xbox, PlayStation and most other standard gamepads, over USB or Bluetooth. Connect the controller in your Mac's Bluetooth settings before starting the game.

Can I change the controls?

Yes. Open Settings > Controls on the title screen to rebind any key.

A level is too hard.

Turn on Assist Mode in Settings. It gives tickets more time and lets you move on to the next level without earning stars.

I'm colorblind.

Settings has a colorblind mode that changes the warning colors on the screen and in the levels to a colorblind-safe set.

Where is my save?

Progress saves automatically on your computer. It is never uploaded anywhere. See the privacy policy.
