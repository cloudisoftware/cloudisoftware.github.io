Overloaded

Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Overloaded collects no personal data.

Overloaded is a local co-op game made by Cloudi Software. It has no accounts, no online features, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The game never connects to the internet.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress (level stars and best scores) and your settings (volume, display, controls, accessibility options) in a file on your own computer. That file never leaves your computer. Deleting the game, or its save data, removes it.

Controllers

The game reads input from game controllers connected over USB or Bluetooth so you can play. It doesn't record or send that input anywhere.

Children

Because the game collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Store purchases

If you bought the game from an app store, that store handles your purchase under its own privacy policy. Cloudi Software receives no personal information about you from it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
